# MOSSlight — Dialogue Library

## Use and continuity notes

These are authored conversations, not mandatory scenes. Every interaction starts because the player chooses to speak, offer a gift, or join an activity. NPCs may be seen in routines or talking to one another, but never approach, chase, interrupt, or penalize the player for declining. A line marked **Discovery** assumes the player has found the named place or clue. Friendship tiers are **New**, **Familiar**, **Friend**, and **Deep Trust**. Do not show a revelation before its trust or discovery condition. Characters can be quiet; silence is a valid variation.

The world leaves the human disappearance and the Mosslight's cause unresolved. Dream statements are not confirmed history. The Continuance is an old monitoring and infrastructure network, not a proven cause of the transformation.

## Keeper of the Pond — Nera

### Everyday life

1. **Morning** — **Player:** “Up early?” **Nera:** “The pond was up earlier. I lost the argument.”
2. **Daytime** — **Nera:** “If you see a jar with a blue thread, don't open it. It contains a smell I haven't named yet.” **Player:** “Is that dangerous?” **Nera:** “Mostly to my confidence.”
3. **Evening** — **Player:** “How was the water?” **Nera:** “Wet. A little proud of itself. The usual.”
4. **Rain** — **Nera:** “Rain makes every channel look like it has a plan. Give it an hour. It doesn't.”
5. **Winter** — **Player:** “The pond's gone quiet.” **Nera:** “Under the skin, it's busy. Ice is just a lid with poor manners.”
6. **Quiet moment** — **Nera:** “That beetle has been trying to climb the same reed for ten minutes.” **Player:** “Should we help?” **Nera:** “It hasn't asked. I respect that.”

### Emotional and social

7. **Comfort** — **Player:** “I made a mess of things.” **Nera:** “Then we have a mess. Sit down. We can decide what it means after you've eaten.”
8. **Funny** — **Player:** “Do fish sleep?” **Nera:** “Yes.” **Player:** “How can you tell?” **Nera:** “They stop judging my posture.”
9. **Vulnerable** — **Nera:** “I keep checking the north bank. There's nothing wrong today.” **Player:** “And yesterday?” **Nera:** “Yesterday I checked it twelve times. I don't recommend keeping score.”
10. **Disagreement** — **Player:** “You can't control every drop.” **Nera:** “I know. I dislike hearing accurate things when I'm busy.”
11. **Affection** — **Nera:** “I set out two cups. It wasn't a test. I just hoped you'd be here.”
12. **Loneliness** — **Player:** “Do you ever get lonely?” **Nera:** “The pond is full of life. That isn't the same as having someone notice you left the bank.”

### Relationship and story

13. **New — first meeting** — **Nera:** “Stop there, please. North-bank water isn't checked yet.” **Player:** “I was just looking.” **Nera:** “Good. Looking is safe. Drinking is a separate decision.”
14. **Familiar** — **Nera:** “You remembered the clean jar.” **Player:** “You asked.” **Nera:** “People remember the dramatic requests. I appreciate the boring ones.”
15. **Friend** — **Nera:** “I have a map of the channels. It is not a map of ownership.” **Player:** “Then why hide it?” **Nera:** “Because I wrote one channel in red. I wanted the page to look less like blame.”
16. **Deep Trust — revelation** — **Nera:** “I diverted the gully water to save my hatch pool. The reeds downstream died.” **Player:** “You were trying to save someone.” **Nera:** “Yes. That doesn't put the reeds back.” **Player:** “No. But we can tell the others and try.” **Nera:** “Stay while I say it. I don't want to make it sound smaller.”
17. **Discovery — pond intake** — **Player:** “There's a pipe under the north bank.” **Nera:** “I know. I was hoping it was an old root.” **Player:** “Roots usually don't have bolts.” **Nera:** “You'd be surprised what this pond has tried.”
18. **Discovery — river mouth** — **Nera:** “It smells different here.” **Player:** “Good different?” **Nera:** “Unfamiliar. That will have to be good enough for today.”

### Gifts, places, events, danger

19. **Gift — clean glass** — **Nera:** “A jar with no cracks. A luxurious little silence.” **Player:** “You like it?” **Nera:** “I do. Thank you.”
20. **Gift — disliked perfume** — **Nera:** “That's… potent.” **Player:** “Bad gift?” **Nera:** “Not bad. Just currently making the pond smell like a flower had a dispute with a cellar.”
21. **Discovery — Low Archive** — **Nera:** “Paper survived down there?” **Player:** “Some of it.” **Nera:** “Then don't read it wet. The dead deserve legible handwriting.”
22. **Campfire** — **Nera:** “I brought tea. It tastes like bark.” **Player:** “Is that the recipe?” **Nera:** “It's the apology.”
23. **Night sound** — **Nera:** “That knock came from below the bank.” **Player:** “Should we check?” **Nera:** “In daylight. Curiosity is not improved by poor visibility.”
24. **Danger** — **Nera:** “Back from the water. Slowly. It is not angry; it is startled. Those are different problems.”
25. **Lantern Walk** — **Nera:** “I left a cup near the path. If no one uses it, I still had a cup to spare.”

## Little Forager — Pip

### Everyday life

1. **Morning** — **Pip:** “I found three berries and one berry-shaped pebble. Breakfast has a strong opening.”
2. **Daytime** — **Player:** “What are you collecting?” **Pip:** “Evidence. Of what? Not sure. That's the exciting bit.”
3. **Evening** — **Pip:** “Do you think crickets get tired of singing the same song?” **Player:** “Maybe.” **Pip:** “Good. I get tired of my songs and I only know four.”
4. **Rain** — **Pip:** “Everything smells like a wet sock. A nice wet sock. Don't tell the flowers I said that.”
5. **Winter** — **Pip:** “My footprints look very serious in snow. Like tiny important meetings.”
6. **Quiet moment** — **Pip:** “I'm trying to sit still.” **Player:** “How's it going?” **Pip:** “I have learned the ground has at least seven textures. So, badly.”

### Emotional and social

7. **Comfort** — **Pip:** “I don't know what to say.” **Player:** “You don't have to.” **Pip:** “Oh. Great. I can do that. I can be quiet at you.”
8. **Funny** — **Pip:** “I named that beetle Captain.” **Player:** “Why?” **Pip:** “It looks like it has a boat. It does not. That's the joke.”
9. **Vulnerable** — **Pip:** “Sometimes I talk fast so nobody has time to notice I don't know the answer.” **Player:** “I notice.” **Pip:** “Yeah. You wait anyway. That's better.”
10. **Disagreement** — **Pip:** “I didn't take it. I borrowed it.” **Player:** “You put it in your pocket.” **Pip:** “A pocket can be a temporary museum.” **Player:** “Ask next time.” **Pip:** “…Okay. Museums need permission.”
11. **Affection** — **Pip:** “I saved you the crunchy bit. I wanted it, so that's how you know it's a big deal.”
12. **Belonging** — **Pip:** “I used to think a home was where you kept your stuff. Now I think it's where people let you put the weird stuff down.”

### Relationship and story

13. **New — first meeting** — **Pip:** “Are you new? You look new. Can I trade you this pebble for a question?” **Player:** “What question?” **Pip:** “Good one. I have to think of it now.”
14. **Familiar** — **Pip:** “I put your shell on the camp board.” **Player:** “Why?” **Pip:** “So I'd remember where I found it. I can take it down.” **Player:** “You can leave it.” **Pip:** “Excellent. Museum open.”
15. **Friend** — **Pip:** “You can read my notes, but don't fix the spelling unless the bug's name changes.”
16. **Deep Trust — hidden page** — **Pip:** “I found this in the root hollow. It says ‘departure’ and then the page stops.” **Player:** “You kept it?” **Pip:** “I hid it. Kept sounds like I had a plan.” **Player:** “Want me to read it with you?” **Pip:** “Yes. Slowly. If it says everybody left, I don't want to hear it alone.”
17. **Discovery — Weather Station** — **Pip:** “The wind vane points at the wall.” **Player:** “Maybe it's broken.” **Pip:** “Maybe the wall is very windy.”
18. **Discovery — Glass Orchard** — **Pip:** “The panes sing! This one goes ‘ting.’ That one goes ‘tang.’ I think the pear tree is the conductor.”

### Gifts, places, events, danger

19. **Gift — snack** — **Pip:** “For me? I was just thinking about food.” **Player:** “You always are.” **Pip:** “That is a hurtful and accurate thing to say.”
20. **Gift — mushy root** — **Pip:** “Is it meant to be this soft?” **Player:** “I think so.” **Pip:** “I'm going to thank you with my face, which is making a brave shape.”
21. **Discovery — Glassling nest** — **Pip:** “It polished my button! It stole my button, then improved it. I have complicated feelings.”
22. **Campfire** — **Pip:** “I have a story about a frog and a spoon.” **Player:** “Is it true?” **Pip:** “The spoon part is. The frog has requested privacy.”
23. **Night sound** — **Pip:** “Something said my sneeze from under the ground.” **Player:** “Maybe an echo.” **Pip:** “I hope so. I don't want to meet a better sneezer.”
24. **Danger** — **Pip:** “I know, I know—back away. I can do careful. I am doing careful with my whole body.”
25. **First frost custom** — **Pip:** “I put a button in the bowl. If someone takes it, I hope they have a shirt with a very specific problem.”

## Lantern Keeper — Sella

### Everyday life

1. **Morning** — **Sella:** “The east lamp stayed lit all night. I think it was showing off.”
2. **Daytime** — **Player:** “What are you fixing?” **Sella:** “A hinge. It has been making a noise like a disappointed aunt.”
3. **Evening** — **Sella:** “The path is clear. Want me to walk with you, or would you rather take the quiet?”
4. **Rain** — **Sella:** “Rain gets into everything. Including my left boot, which is an impressive place for water to find.”
5. **Winter** — **Player:** “Your lamps look warmer tonight.” **Sella:** “Cold makes a small flame look ambitious.”
6. **Quiet moment** — **Sella:** “Listen. No, not for anything. Just listen.” **Player:** “I hear the lamp.” **Sella:** “That's enough.”

### Emotional and social

7. **Comfort** — **Sella:** “You can sit here. We don't have to make the feeling useful.”
8. **Funny** — **Player:** “You have a favorite lamp?” **Sella:** “No.” **Player:** “That one?” **Sella:** “It has a very good personality.”
9. **Vulnerable** — **Sella:** “I keep the lamps lit because I like knowing someone could come back. Some nights I forget that no one asked me to wait.”
10. **Disagreement** — **Player:** “You don't have to fix everything for everyone.” **Sella:** “I know. I just don't always know what to do with my hands when I stop.”
11. **Affection** — **Sella:** “I made the tea the way you like it. You don't have to stay. I wanted to make it.”
12. **Hope** — **Player:** “What do you hope for?” **Sella:** “A lamp that runs on wind. And one evening when I can leave it to do its job.”

### Relationship and story

13. **New — first meeting** — **Sella:** “This path gets slick past the third post. I can show you, if you'd like.” **Player:** “I can find it.” **Sella:** “Of course. The offer stands without following you.”
14. **Familiar** — **Sella:** “I left the low lamp on. You said bright light gives you a headache.” **Player:** “You remembered.” **Sella:** “I did. It's a nice thing to remember.”
15. **Friend** — **Sella:** “There's one lamp I never light.” **Player:** “Why?” **Sella:** “I was told to leave one dark. It felt kind. I still don't know who said it.”
16. **Deep Trust** — **Sella:** “That voice might have been a recording. It might have been someone beside me. I have kept the wick all these years as if it could decide.” **Player:** “What do you think now?” **Sella:** “I think I can keep the wick and stop asking it.”
17. **Story — wind lamp** — **Sella:** “It turned!” **Player:** “Then stopped.” **Sella:** “Yes. We have built a lamp with a very brief career. I'm proud of it.”
18. **Discovery — Lantern Row lights** — **Player:** “I saw lights moving in the fog.” **Sella:** “Did you follow?” **Player:** “No.” **Sella:** “Good. Sometimes the kindest thing is letting a light have its own errand.”

### Gifts, places, events, danger

19. **Gift — tool** — **Sella:** “This fits my hand perfectly. Thank you. I'll try not to use it to fix things that aren't broken.”
20. **Gift — bright glass** — **Sella:** “It's beautiful, but it catches too much light for the path. Could I keep it by my workbench instead?”
21. **Discovery — Weather Station** — **Sella:** “It smells like hot rain in there.” **Player:** “There wasn't any.” **Sella:** “I know. That's why I came back out.”
22. **Campfire** — **Sella:** “I brought mending. You can talk over it, or not. Cloth doesn't mind either way.”
23. **Strange sound** — **Sella:** “That was not a lamp.” **Player:** “Are you scared?” **Sella:** “Yes. I'm going to be scared over here, where the ground is solid.”
24. **Danger** — **Sella:** “Stay behind the stone marker. I have the lamp; you have the clear path. We can both do our part.”
25. **Lantern Walk** — **Sella:** “Hang one if you want. Watch if you want. There's no count at the end.”

## Rootbound Elder — Orun

### Everyday life

1. **Morning** — **Orun:** “A woodpecker inspected me. I failed to provide anything worth eating.”
2. **Daytime** — **Player:** “How old are you?” **Orun:** “Older than that question. Younger than this hill. Somewhere between, probably.”
3. **Evening** — **Orun:** “The shadows are long. Good. It means the sun is going somewhere.”
4. **Rain** — **Orun:** “My knees are drinking.” **Player:** “You have knees?” **Orun:** “Two excellent root-knots. Don't be rude.”
5. **Winter** — **Orun:** “Snow is a blanket with no understanding of furniture.”
6. **Quiet moment** — **Orun:** “That moss is growing on the north face.” **Player:** “Does that matter?” **Orun:** “Not especially. I enjoy being right about small things.”

### Emotional and social

7. **Comfort** — **Orun:** “Come sit. No advice today. I have used up my quota.”
8. **Funny** — **Player:** “Are you asleep?” **Orun:** “No.” **Player:** “You were snoring.” **Orun:** “The beetles were moving a log. It was a group effort.”
9. **Vulnerable** — **Orun:** “They ask me what I remember. Sometimes I remember being tired. That is not the kind of old story people want.”
10. **Disagreement** — **Player:** “We should open the door.” **Orun:** “Perhaps. You are speaking as though the door owes us an answer.” **Player:** “It doesn't.” **Orun:** “Good. Then we can go carefully.”
11. **Affection** — **Orun:** “I grew this branch low. It makes a fine seat. You may use it without making a speech.”
12. **Belonging** — **Player:** “Do you miss the past?” **Orun:** “I miss particular things. A bird. A smell after rain. I do not miss being a past for other people.”

### Relationship and story

13. **New — first meeting** — **Orun:** “Would you move left? A root is growing where your foot is.” **Player:** “I don't see it.” **Orun:** “It is very young. It has not learned to be seen.”
14. **Familiar** — **Orun:** “The path you asked about is passable. Avoid the shiny mud. Shiny mud is a liar.”
15. **Friend** — **Orun:** “There is a door in the Root Rooms. I have let roots cover it.” **Player:** “Because something's inside?” **Orun:** “Because I do not know. Fear can be very tidy.”
16. **Deep Trust — at the door** — **Orun:** “I will not ask you to open it. I am asking you to stand here while I decide whether I want to.” **Player:** “I'm here.” **Orun:** “Good. That's a useful kind of company.”
17. **Story — Glasslings** — **Orun:** “The clicking was a nest. Small creatures, not a machine.” **Player:** “Does that disappoint you?” **Orun:** “No. It is a relief with tiny feet.”
18. **Discovery — old mark** — **Player:** “The symbol is on Morrow, too.” **Orun:** “Then the mark traveled. It does not tell us who carried it.”

### Gifts, places, events, danger

19. **Gift — compost** — **Orun:** “Excellent. This is a gift that knows what it is for.”
20. **Gift — bell** — **Orun:** “It is very loud.” **Player:** “You don't like it?” **Orun:** “I like that you thought of me. I do not like the bell. Both may be true.”
21. **Discovery — Glass Orchard** — **Orun:** “The panes sing because they are cracked.” **Player:** “That's sad.” **Orun:** “It is also how they sing. Let a thing have more than one fact.”
22. **Campfire** — **Orun:** “I knew a story once about a squirrel and a bucket.” **Player:** “What happened?” **Orun:** “The bucket was fine. The squirrel was very wet.”
23. **Night knock** — **Orun:** “One knock from below. I answered once. Now we wait.”
24. **Danger — dry wind** — **Orun:** “No flame. No sparks. The roots are thirsty enough to take offense.”
25. **First frost bowl** — **Orun:** “I put in a handful of soil. It is not useful to eat, but it is good to remember where food grows.”

## Mechanical Wanderer — Morrow

### Everyday life

1. **Morning** — **Morrow:** “Good morning. My left wheel is making a new sound. I have classified it as ‘probably conversational.’”
2. **Daytime** — **Player:** “What are you doing?” **Morrow:** “Following a bird. It has not provided a route plan. I continue anyway.”
3. **Evening** — **Morrow:** “The temperature is dropping. I recommend a coat.” **Player:** “You don't wear one.” **Morrow:** “I am attempting empathy through analogy.”
4. **Rain** — **Morrow:** “I am under the roof. This is not a request for rescue. It is a report on my successful planning.”
5. **Winter** — **Morrow:** “My wheel has frozen. I am not distressed. I am… expressing a strong preference for thaw.”
6. **Quiet moment** — **Morrow:** “A thrush repeated the same phrase four times. I understood none of it. I found the repetition reassuring.”

### Emotional and social

7. **Comfort** — **Player:** “I feel useless.” **Morrow:** “That is a feeling, not a measurement. I can remain here while it passes, if you prefer.”
8. **Funny** — **Player:** “Can you dance?” **Morrow:** “Yes.” **Morrow:** “That was a wheel calibration. I apologize for the confidence.”
9. **Vulnerable** — **Morrow:** “I remember being activated. I do not remember who did it. I dislike that the missing part feels important.”
10. **Disagreement** — **Player:** “Let me repair you.” **Morrow:** “No.” **Player:** “I can help.” **Morrow:** “You may offer help. You may not convert my refusal into a faulty setting.”
11. **Affection** — **Morrow:** “I have recorded the time you usually arrive. This is not surveillance. It is… anticipation. I believe.”
12. **Hope** — **Player:** “What do you want?” **Morrow:** “A task I choose. A route I have not been assigned. Perhaps a name that does not sound like a label.”

### Relationship and story

13. **New — first meeting** — **Morrow:** “Please move the stone beside your foot. It obstructs my route.” **Player:** “Where are you going?” **Morrow:** “I am revising that plan.”
14. **Familiar** — **Morrow:** “You usually take the lower path. Today you chose the ridge. I noticed. I am not asking why.”
15. **Friend** — **Player:** “Does M-4 mean anything?” **Morrow:** “It appears to be a designation. I prefer Morrow. Pip selected it after I said I had no preference. I had a preference.”
16. **Deep Trust — signal** — **Morrow:** “The signal I followed may be my own diagnostic pulse.” **Player:** “Does that change anything?” **Morrow:** “Yes. It means I may have traveled this far for no instruction. I find that… encouraging.”
17. **Story — shutdown test** — **Morrow:** “I will power down for six minutes. You do not need to stay.” **Player:** “I know.” **Morrow:** “I would like you to, if you choose.”
18. **Discovery — Root Room relay** — **Morrow:** “The mark matches. The relay has no command, only a partial route and a date with two digits missing.” **Player:** “Can you restore it?” **Morrow:** “No. I can stop pretending that restoration is the only useful outcome.”

### Gifts, places, events, danger

19. **Gift — spare fastener** — **Morrow:** “A correct size. You observed my wheel.” **Player:** “I did.” **Morrow:** “Thank you. I will install it when I want to.”
20. **Gift — soft cloth** — **Morrow:** “This is not mechanically necessary.” **Player:** “You can still have it.” **Morrow:** “I will place it over my casing. For… comfort.”
21. **Discovery — Weather Station** — **Morrow:** “The relay clicked twice, paused, then once. It resembles a pattern I do not remember learning.”
22. **Campfire** — **Morrow:** “I cannot taste the stew.” **Player:** “You can still sit with us.” **Morrow:** “I know. I am practicing sitting without claiming a function.”
23. **Night sound** — **Morrow:** “That sound came from below us. I cannot identify it. I will not invent certainty to improve the silence.”
24. **Danger — Hush Hounds** — **Morrow:** “Stop moving. They are tracking vibration. We will wait until the ground is quiet, then leave together.”
25. **Lantern Walk** — **Morrow:** “I have hung a lamp. It is not efficient illumination. It is a visible sign that the route can be shared.”

## Dreaming Creature — Luma

### Everyday life

1. **Morning** — **Luma:** “Good morning. I dreamed I was a very small hill. A rabbit sat on me. I liked having a job.”
2. **Daytime** — **Player:** “Were you asleep?” **Luma:** “I was resting my eyes from the inside.”
3. **Evening** — **Luma:** “The sky looks like it forgot what color it wanted. I like that about it.”
4. **Rain** — **Luma:** “Rain on leaves sounds like tiny feet going somewhere important. Probably home. I hope they packed snacks.”
5. **Winter** — **Player:** “Cold?” **Luma:** “A little. I have tucked my paws under myself. I am now a warm comma.”
6. **Quiet moment** — **Luma:** “I lined up these seed pods by size. Now I don't remember what the size was supposed to tell me.”

### Emotional and social

7. **Comfort** — **Luma:** “You don't need to explain the whole hurt. You can give me the small edge of it, if that's easier.”
8. **Funny** — **Luma:** “I dreamed the moon was a potato.” **Player:** “Was it delicious?” **Luma:** “No, it was tired. Please respect its privacy.”
9. **Vulnerable** — **Luma:** “Sometimes I wake up and the place looks unfamiliar for one breath. Then I remember it. That one breath is very long.”
10. **Disagreement** — **Player:** “Your dream must mean something.” **Luma:** “Maybe. But I don't want it turned into homework.” **Player:** “You're right. Sorry.” **Luma:** “Thank you. We can let it be odd.”
11. **Affection** — **Luma:** “I saved the warm stone for you. It isn't warm anymore. I thought we could warm it again together.”
12. **Loneliness** — **Player:** “Do you feel alone?” **Luma:** “Yes. Also, sometimes I want everyone to go away. Both feelings fit. It's a crowded little room.”

### Relationship and story

13. **New — first meeting** — **Luma:** “Are you a tree?” **Player:** “No.” **Luma:** “Good. I was about to apologize for sleeping on you.”
14. **Familiar** — **Luma:** “I dreamed you brought a pear. You didn't. I am telling you this as a very unfair review of reality.”
15. **Friend** — **Luma:** “Do you want a dream story, or a normal one?” **Player:** “Normal.” **Luma:** “A beetle stole a seed pod. It came back for a second one. I respected the ambition.”
16. **Deep Trust — recurring dream** — **Luma:** “Green room. Ticking. A door too small for me.” **Player:** “Do you want to look for it?” **Luma:** “Not today. Could you just sit with me while I wake up properly?” **Player:** “Yes.” **Luma:** “Good. I am here now. I can feel the stone.”
17. **Discovery — Low Archive panel** — **Player:** “There's a green room painted behind the shelf.” **Luma:** “Oh.” **Player:** “Does it explain the dream?” **Luma:** “It explains why I know that shade. Not why I dream it. That's enough.”
18. **Discovery — Second Dawn** — **Luma:** “The world glowed under the hill.” **Player:** “Do you know why?” **Luma:** “No. I was hoping you did. We can both be surprised.”

### Gifts, places, events, danger

19. **Gift — pear** — **Luma:** “A pear. This is an excellent real object. It has no hidden symbolism that I can detect.”
20. **Gift — sharp bell** — **Luma:** “Thank you. It is too sharp for me, but I can leave it with Sella. She'll know what to do.”
21. **Discovery — Glass Orchard** — **Luma:** “I sleep here when the fruit is ripe. Everything smells like a sweet thing thinking very hard.”
22. **Campfire** — **Luma:** “I have a story, but it doesn't go anywhere.” **Player:** “That's fine.” **Luma:** “A stone was warm. Then it wasn't. The end.”
23. **Night Choir** — **Luma:** “The frogs sound like someone trying to remember a song without waking the singer.”
24. **Danger — Hush Hounds** — **Luma:** “Don't run. My heart is already doing that for all of us.”
25. **First frost bowl** — **Luma:** “I put in a smooth stone. Take it if your pocket needs a little weight.”

## Cross-character conversations

These ambient exchanges may be overheard if the player chooses to linger nearby. They do not trigger or address the player automatically.

1. **Pip and Nera, at the pond** — **Pip:** “If I put a berry in the water, will it float?” **Nera:** “Possibly.” **Pip:** “And if it sinks?” **Nera:** “Then you have fed the pond.” **Pip:** “I like a snack with options.”
2. **Sella and Morrow, repairing a lamp** — **Morrow:** “The wick is crooked.” **Sella:** “So am I, some mornings.” **Morrow:** “Should I correct you?” **Sella:** “No.” **Morrow:** “Understood. I will not correct either of us.”
3. **Orun and Luma, dusk** — **Luma:** “Your roots make a good wall.” **Orun:** “They are not a wall.” **Luma:** “A wall with excellent manners, then.” **Orun:** “That is more accurate.”
4. **Pip and Morrow, on the path** — **Pip:** “Race you to the post!” **Morrow:** “I am not optimized for competition.” **Pip:** “So that's a yes?” **Morrow:** “That is an incomplete refusal.”
5. **Nera and Sella, after rain** — **Sella:** “You look tired.” **Nera:** “I am inspecting the bank.” **Sella:** “You can inspect it sitting down.” **Nera:** “That is annoyingly practical.” **Sella:** “I learned from the best.”
6. **Group at the Empty Bowl** — **Pip:** “Someone put in a button.” **Morrow:** “It has four holes. Potentially useful.” **Orun:** “Or potentially a button.” **Luma:** “I like that it doesn't have to decide.” **Nera:** “The bowl can hold both.”

## Choice responses

Use these short acknowledgements when the player declines, leaves, or chooses not to pursue a thread. They preserve warmth without guilt.

- **Nera:** “All right. The water will still be here.”
- **Pip:** “Okay! I'll keep the question. It can wait in my pocket.”
- **Sella:** “Of course. I'll leave the lamp low.”
- **Orun:** “Good. A choice made slowly is still a choice.”
- **Morrow:** “Acknowledged. No penalty recorded. I mean that literally.”
- **Luma:** “That's fine. We can let the moment stay unfinished.”
